## Root cause (proven)
Tenant mismatch. Jacopo's production agency = tenant `c5ae2d0e-283e-4513-aaf2-cbd97ef62d78` (26,414 policies, all current users, all recent uploads). The Google Drive connection + initialized folder structure exist ONLY under a different, older/demo tenant `tenant-principale-schiantarelli` (linked by admin@assicura.it). Because `cloud_connections` for tenant `c5ae2d0e…` does not exist and `cloud_folder_cache` for it is empty, the backend stores uploads internally (`storage_provider="emergent"`), never mirrors them to Drive, and never creates an INBOX folder — exactly the two reported symptoms. No encryption-key or refresh-token issue is involved for this tenant.

## Suggested fixes
1. USER (primary): Jacopo must log in with his real production account `jacopo@schiantarelli.it` (tenant `c5ae2d0e…`) and (re)connect Google Drive from Librerie → Cloud Storage. This creates a `cloud_connections` row and initializes the INBOX/folder structure under the correct tenant. He must NOT connect while logged in as the gmail/demo account (`schiantarelli.jacopo@gmail.com` / tenant-principale-schiantarelli) — that is what produced the misbound connection. After reconnecting, new uploads should mirror; existing `storage_provider="emergent"` attachments will not retroactively sync.
2. CODE (hardening, optional): the app silently stored uploads internally with no `pending_sync` and no user-visible warning when the tenant had no Drive connection. Surface a clear "no cloud storage connected for this agency" state, and consider a backfill/re-sync job to push already-stored `emergent` attachments once a connection is established.
3. Alternative (requires writing prod data — NOT done here): re-point the existing connection's tenant from `tenant-principale-schiantarelli` to `c5ae2d0e…`. Risky (folder 188eaRaNW7… belongs to the Google account that admin@assicura.it authorized; may not be the account jacopo wants). Reconnect (fix #1) is cleaner and safer.
